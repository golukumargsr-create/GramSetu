GramSetu: Democratizing Rural Essential Infrastructure

Over 65% of India's population lives in rural areas where irregular water tankers, unannounced power cuts, and chronic medicine shortages lead to economic loss and avoidable deaths. GramSetu bridges the last-mile digital divide.

🔴 The 3 Rural Pain Points Solved:
  • Water: Villagers waste 3+ hours waiting for unscheduled water tankers or walk to dry boreholes.
  • Electricity: Random 4-hour blackouts ruin crop irrigation, milk chilling, and baby nebulizers.
  • Healthcare: Pregnant women and diabetes patients cycle 15 km only to find PHC medicine racks empty.
🟢 The GramSetu Innovation:
  • Zero-Internet Offline-First: Automatic LocalStorage queue + 2G Feature Phone SMS & IVR integration.
  • Voice-First AI: Illiterate elderly citizens speak in Hindi/English to lodge reports or check stock.
  • Hyperlocal Community P2P: Urja Sangam connects families needing emergency electricity with solar neighbors.
